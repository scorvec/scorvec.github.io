City temperature forecasts

Daily high and low temperatures for 234 US cities, fourteen days ahead, from a calibrated blend of nine global and regional weather models and their 152 ensemble members. Pick a day and a city to see the forecast, the spread and how each model differs.

loading…

The headline forecast is a purpose-built blending model that combines the global and regional models by lead time, location and season, learning from how each has verified. The blend is proprietary; the individual models it draws on are shown alongside for comparison, and the verification page scores every one of them, the blend included, against observed highs and lows.
