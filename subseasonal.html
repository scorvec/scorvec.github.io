How it is built

Anomalies are against the 1991–2020 normal. Each forecast is differenced from a lead-dependent GEPS8 hindcast climatology (2001–2020, read at the init day-of-year), which removes the model's drift and its mean bias together; the reference is then moved to 1991–2020 by the observed ERA5 shift between the two periods, with no model in that step. The stratosphere, which the hindcast does not cover, is taken against ERA5 with the model bias measured rather than assumed. Teleconnection probabilities, tercile maps and the MJO score are calibrated on the same hindcast, and the skill masks hatch wherever its anomaly correlation falls below 0.25. Read the full method →
