Atmospheric dynamics explainers, with live forecasts

The diagnostics behind this site's circulation and stratosphere products, each explained with its equations and shown with its current forecast. They are written for meteorologists and students who want to know what a chart is measuring before trusting it.

  • Eliassen–Palm flux and E–P flux divergence

    What the Eliassen–Palm (E–P) flux and its divergence measure, how to read an E–P cross-section, and a live 15-day GEFS forecast of the wave driving up to 1 hPa.

  • Mountain torque and friction torque

    How mountain and friction torques trade angular momentum between the Earth and the atmosphere, how to read torque maps, and live AIFS-ENS forecasts by range.

  • Atmospheric angular momentum (AAM)

    What atmospheric angular momentum measures, why it tracks El Niño and the length of day, the GWO phase space, and a live AIFS-ENS forecast by hemisphere.

  • Wave activity flux (Takaya–Nakamura)

    What the Takaya–Nakamura wave activity flux shows about Rossby wave packets, downstream development and blocking, and a live AIFS-ENS forecast at 250 hPa.

  • 100 hPa eddy heat flux

    Why the 100 hPa poleward eddy heat flux measures planetary waves entering the stratosphere, how its 40-day mean leads the vortex, and a live AIFS-ENS forecast.

  • Sudden stratospheric warmings and the polar vortex

    What a sudden stratospheric warming is, splits and displacements, how SSWs reach the surface, every major SSW since 1980, and a live polar vortex forecast.

  • Hadley cell and the mean meridional mass streamfunction

    How the mean meridional mass streamfunction describes the Hadley, Ferrel and polar cells, how their edges are measured, and a live ECMWF analysis.

  • Dynamic tropopause and potential vorticity (2 PVU)

    What the dynamic tropopause is, how to read θ on the 2-PVU surface and potential vorticity on 330 and 350 K, and live AIFS forecast maps to day 10.
