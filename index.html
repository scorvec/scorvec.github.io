Meteorologist and atmospheric scientist

Numerical weather prediction, operational forecasting, and the weather behind energy markets. This site runs a set of live diagnostics built from open model and observation data.

See what is running

Live products, rebuilt automatically as new model runs and observations arrive.

Everything here is computed from public data (ECMWF and Environment Canada open data, NOAA, NASA, IDEAM) by pipelines that run on a schedule. Each page says when it was last updated; the freshness board shows the whole set at a glance.

Tropics and El NiñoOcean and atmosphere, from the daily state to the seasonal outlook

  • El Niño monitorDaily ONI and RONI, Niño-region sea surface temperatures, and SST anomaly maps and animation.Daily
  • Subsurface temperatureEquatorial Pacific depth–longitude sections from the TAO/TRITON array, raw and with the climate trend removed.Daily
  • ENSO forecastsEvery member of seven centres' seasonal models for Niño-3.4, with percentile fans and a record of how past forecasts did.Monthly
  • Atmospheric responseWave activity flux, angular momentum and torques, and the Walker circulation from the AIFS ensemble.Daily
  • MJO forecastReal-time multivariate MJO index from the ECMWF AIFS ensemble, with the observed phase-space track.Twice daily
  • Subseasonal outlookGEPS weeks 1 to 5 for the Americas, the tropics and the polar vortex, with change since the previous run.Mondays and Thursdays
  • QBO trackerEquatorial stratospheric winds at 10 to 100 hPa from radiosondes, 1950 to the present.Weekly

Convection and observationsShort-range convective diagnostics and raw observations

  • Entraining CAPEGridded ECAPE over the continental US from the 3 km HRRR, hourly to 18 hours and 3-hourly to 48.Four times daily
  • Sounding explorerReal-time and archived radiosondes worldwide, drawn and analysed in the browser, with launch history and record rings.Hourly mirror
  • Five-minute airport observationsTemperature at major US airports every five minutes from ASOS, with running daily highs and lows.Live
  • Columbia River basin precipitationSeven models' precipitation on the NWRFC water-supply divisions, as percent of normal and against Stage IV observations.Four times daily

SeasonalBeyond a month

  • ECMWF SEAS5 outlookNiño indices from 1+2 to 4 against the previous issues, PDO and Atlantic indices, calibrated tercile maps over the Americas, and polar-cap stratospheric heights.Monthly
  • NOAA SFS outlookMaps from NOAA's new Seasonal Forecast System, 31 members, for the months ahead.Monthly

Model verificationHow the models are doing, measured against observations

  • AIFS single versus ensemble controlLive verification of the two ECMWF AIFS configurations against radiosondes and ERA5, run by run.Every 00Z and 12Z run
  • HRRR and RRFS kinetic-energy spectraEffective resolution and mesoscale spin-up of the two US convection-allowing models, from their native-level winds.Study
