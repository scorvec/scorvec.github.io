Product freshness

Every product on the site and when it last updated, read in this browser from the same files the pages load. On time is within the product's normal cadence; late is past it (usually a queued job); stale is more than twice over. Refreshes itself every five minutes.

Timestamps are the render/issue time each product records in its own manifest or data file (ver, generated, updated, init…). Products that publish only an image carry no timestamp, so their last commit is asked of the GitHub API instead - that has a 60/hour unauthenticated budget and is cached here for ten minutes. Forecast products dated by model init (GDPS, ECAPE) are naturally a few hours behind the clock; their cadence allows for it. Date-only products (radar, visitor stats) are judged at the end of the day they name.
