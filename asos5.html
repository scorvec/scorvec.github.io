Click a card to isolate a station (adds its official 24-hour max/min lines, or a 6-hourly reconstruction when the daily group isn’t posted) · times are each station’s local clock
Primary source: the official NWS API (api.weather.gov), fetched directly in your browser on every visit — it serves 5-minute ASOS observations for 13 of these stations and hourly METARs for KJFK & KNYC (marked “hourly”), so the page is always current with no server job in the loop. The daily max/min are the absolute extremes since local midnight: the sampled observations merged with the official METAR 6-hour max/min temperature groups (the 1sTTT/2sTTT remark groups, parsed from each hourly METAR) when those exceed the samples; when an extreme comes from a 6-hour group the card shows the group’s time window instead of an exact minute. As a precision overlay, the 0.1 °C NOAA/NWS MADIS HFMETAR 5-minute feed is layered in when the background updater has posted it recently. When a station is isolated, its official 24-hour max/min (the 4… daily-summary group) is drawn as dashed reference lines; if that group isn’t posted, the lines are reconstructed from the four 6-hour groups over the last 24 h and shown dotted with a “6-hr” tag. Obviously bad values are dropped.
