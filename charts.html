48-Hour US Wind Generation Forecast

Per-turbine wind power forecast for every wind turbine in the United States, summed by ISO and balancing authority. The pipeline starts from ~75,000 turbine locations in the USGS Wind Turbine Database, samples HRRR 80-meter wind speeds at each turbine's exact lat/lon, applies a model-specific power curve (windpowerlib catalog where available, NREL WIND Toolkit specific-power generic curves otherwise), and corrects for hub-height and air density. The forecast represents potential generation; observed output is typically lower due to curtailment and outages.

Model Performance vs. Actuals (14-day rolling)

Day-ahead forecasts from this model (12–24 hour lead) plotted against actual generation and reported curtailment for the three ISOs that publish curtailment data. The blue line is the model's potential-generation forecast; the green dashed line subtracts ISO-reported curtailment to give the deliverable expectation; the red line is what actually showed up on the grid. Persistent gap between the green and red lines indicates residual model error after accounting for curtailment.

Plant-Level Forecast Map

Every utility-scale wind plant in the US fleet (≥5 MW) shown on a map, colored by capacity factor and sized by nameplate. Drag the slider to step through the 48-hour forecast, or hit play to animate. Hover any plant for current generation and details. Click a plant to see its full 48-hour time-series in the panel below the map. Pinch or scroll to zoom; drag to pan. Useful for spotting regional ramps, identifying which plants drive ISO totals, and seeing how a weather pattern plays across the fleet.
