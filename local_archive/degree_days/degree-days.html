Degree-Day Surprise Tracker

In energy weather the forecast itself is rarely the story — the revision is. This page tracks population-weighted heating and cooling degree days for the USA, Canada, Brazil and Argentina from every ECMWF HRES and AIFS-ENS cycle (00Z and 12Z), and shows how the 10-day totals have moved run over run. Europe and Asia are next.

Daily degree days & the run-over-run revision

Left column: the newest cycle's daily CDD (bars up, red) and HDD (bars down, blue) from the AIFS-ENS ensemble mean, with HRES as the dotted line — where they disagree, the physics and AI models see different demand. Right column: the surprise — the days-1–10 total for each index re-plotted at every archived init time. A rising CDD line means successive runs keep adding cooling demand; flat lines mean the market's picture hasn't changed. Both models are computed on the same metro population weights and 18.3 °C (65 °F) base, so systematic bias cancels in the revisions.

ECMWF open data: AIFS-ENS ensemble-mean 2m temperature (6-hourly, day 15) · HRES 2m temperature (6-hourly, day 10) · population-weighted metros (25 US / 10 CA / 14 BR / 8 AR) · base 65°F / 18.3°C · updated after each 00Z/12Z cycle
