Canada's Hybrid AI Model

In spring 2026 Environment and Climate Change Canada launched a hybrid forecast system: the operational GDPS (GEM dynamics, 15 km) is spectrally nudged toward the large-scale temperature and wind fields of GEML, the Global Environmental eMuLator — ECCC's data-driven AI weather model (28 km, 13 pressure levels, 10-day range, on Datamart since March 2026). This page shows the raw AI emulator side by side with the operational model it steers: where the two agree, the hybrid simply sharpens; where they diverge, the nudging is doing real work.

500 hPa height — GEML vs GDPS, day 0–10

Left: GDPS-GEML, the AI emulator's own free-running forecast. Right: the operational GDPS. Same cycle, same colour scale, every 6 hours through hour 240. Watch the AI's smoother-but-steadier large scales against the NWP's sharper synoptic detail — the exact trade the GDPS-SN hybrid is built to arbitrage. Refreshed daily from the 00Z cycle.

ECCC MSC Datamart (open government licence) · GDPS-GEML 0.25° · GDPS 0.15° · both shown as 500 hPa height (dam), NH polar stereographic · 41 frames per cycle

About GEML and GDPS-SN. GEML is an experimental deterministic emulator (six atmospheric variables on 13 pressure levels plus surface fields) run twice daily to 10 days. The operational hybrid, GDPS-SN, does not replace GEM with AI — it keeps the full physics model and nudges only the large scales toward GEML, retaining physically consistent precipitation, clouds and surface weather while borrowing the AI's large-scale skill. A deterministic verification of GEML against ERA5 (alongside the AIFS single-vs-control benchmark) is planned as the archive accumulates.
