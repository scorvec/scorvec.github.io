Canada's Hybrid AI Model

Three models to keep straight. GEM was Canada's pure-physics global model. GEML (Global Environmental eMuLator) is ECCC's data-driven AI weather model (28 km, 13 pressure levels, 10 days). And since spring 2026 the operational GDPS is the hybrid: GEM with its large-scale temperature and winds spectrally nudged toward GEML every step — the pure un-nudged model is no longer published. So every "GDPS" panel below is the hybrid GDPS‑GEML forecast, and the comparison against the free-running raw emulator shows exactly what the nudging keeps and what it overrides.

Synoptic pairs — MSLP, thickness & precipitation type

About the models. GEML is an experimental deterministic emulator (six atmospheric variables on 13 pressure levels plus surface fields) run twice daily to 10 days on Datamart as model_gdps-geml. The hybrid does not replace GEM with AI — it keeps the full physics model and nudges only the large-scale temperature and winds toward GEML, retaining physically consistent precipitation, clouds and surface weather while borrowing the AI's large-scale skill; that hybrid is the operational GDPS (model_gdps) shown here. A deterministic verification of GEML against ERA5 (alongside the AIFS single-vs-control benchmark) is planned as the archive accumulates.
