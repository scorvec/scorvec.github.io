Page guides

The product pages show numbers and figures; what each figure shows, how it is made and what it is built from is written up here, one guide a page. The physics behind the diagnostics is in the explainers.

  • El Niño monitor

    What each figure of the El Niño monitor shows and how it is made: the daily ONI and RONI estimates and their grading, the satellite views, the winds and the Southern Oscillation, the subsurface ocean and the impacts on the Americas.

  • Circulation page

    What each figure of the circulation page shows and how it is made: the dynamic tropopause, the North Pacific jet and its branches, wave activity flux and teleconnections, the Walker and Hadley circulations, tropical rain, the subtropical jets, atmospheric angular momentum and the surface torques.

  • Stratosphere page

    What each figure of the stratosphere page shows and how it is made: the polar vortex and the chance of a sudden warming, the waves that drive it, the descent to the surface, weeks 3 to 5, the season ahead, the QBO and the record of past winters.

  • Tropical waves page

    What each figure of the tropical waves page shows and how it is made: the filtered Kelvin, equatorial Rossby and MJO signals along the equator, the wave trackers, the Kelvin-wave forecast and the ocean's Kelvin waves.

  • Atmospheric rivers page

    What each figure of the atmospheric rivers page shows and how it is made: integrated vapour transport in four models, the West Coast landfall views, the ensembles' AR odds, weeks 3 to 5 and precipitable water.
