Source. IDEAM radar via AWS Open Data (s3-radaresideam), which carries raw SIGMET volumes only — no QPE, CAPPI or composite products exist upstream, so everything here is derived with Py-ART.

Scan strategy. One volume is split across four files on a ~5 minute cycle (1.3° surveillance; 1.5/2.4/3.1/5.1; 6.4/8; 10/12.5/15). These loops use the 1.3° surveillance sweep only — mixing tasks makes the beam tilt rather than the weather move.

Quality control. Gates are kept only where dual-pol RhoHV > 0.85 and coherent power > 0.30, then despeckled. This removes roughly 80% of raw echo, nearly all clutter and biota. Speckle at the radar site is residual clutter in the cone of silence.

Latency. The bucket lands in complete daily batches, so data is about a day behind — this is an archive, not a nowcast. Gold outlines are the hydro basins used by the inflow model.
