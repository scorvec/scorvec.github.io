Brazil Hydro — ONS/SIN monitor

Sibling of the Colombia hydro project, built on ONS open data: ENA (natural inflow energy) and EAR (stored energy) daily by basin, 2000–present, with ONS's own long-term-mean (MLT) norms. Rain products, per-basin kernel models and AIFS+IFS ensemble fans follow the Colombia architecture and are under construction. This page is unlinked and provisional.

Rain → ENA models + ensemble forecasts

Each basin's IMERG rainfall through fitted memory kernels (τ up to 120 days for the continental basins — São Francisco carries a 90-day memory) plus the EAR storage state, targeting ENA as % of MLT. The orange fans are ~101 bias-corrected AIFS-ENS + IFS-ENS members; bias factors and blend weights are measured from a 3-week verification archive (4,700+ pairs) and update twice daily. Paraná mainstem is structurally weak (routed upstream water + Itaipu regulation, not local rain).
