Tropical waves

The waves that organise tropical rain: Kelvin waves, equatorial Rossby waves, mixed Rossby–gravity waves and the MJO. The spectrum shows how the last 96 days of tropical cloudiness divide among them; the Hovmöllers and trackers show where each one is along the equator and where the forecast takes it. The MJO has its own page, MJO forecast.
