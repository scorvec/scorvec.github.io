Monthly, from the Copernicus C3S multi-system seasonal ensemble

Seasonal outlook by model

Eight seasonal systems from seven centres, one at a time or averaged: global anomaly maps for temperature, precipitation, pressure and sea-surface temperature, beside the Niño-3.4 plume that carries every one of their members. Each model is shown against its own 1993–2016 hindcast, so the systems are comparable even though their biases are not. ECMWF SEAS5 is one of the models here; its member-level products — calibrated terciles, threshold days, precipitation minus evaporation — live on the SEAS5 page, which is the only system whose full ensemble is held locally.
