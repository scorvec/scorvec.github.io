About this page

Looking for SFS model plots? This page is an independent visualization suite for NOAA's new SFS (Seasonal Forecast System) — the UFS-based replacement for CFSv2, running in near-real-time beta since March 2026. NOAA publishes the raw ensemble output openly but (as of now) few graphical products; everything here — the Niño-3.4/RONI plumes, global anomaly maps, monthly map loops and the IOD/SASD/PDO indices — is computed directly from the full 31-member zarr output and refreshed with each monthly issue.

About the system. SFS beta is a NOAA research prototype (published by the 6th of each month), not yet an operational product — ensemble size, physics and post-processing are still evolving, and months may occasionally be late or revised. The reforecast climatology here uses 11 members per year over 1991–2020, init-month specific, so lead-dependent drift cancels by construction. Data: NOAA Open Data Dissemination, noaa-oar-sfsdev-pds on AWS.

SFS beta data: NOAA/OAR via NODD (public domain) · C3S overlay: Copernicus Climate Change Service · observed indices: NOAA CPC ERSSTv5, BoM RMM.

What this page reads, and how each figure is built

NOAA's next-generation Seasonal Forecast System — the UFS-based successor to CFSv2, running in near-real-time beta since March 2026 with open zarr output. Every month: 31 members, 12 monthly leads. Anomalies are computed against the model's own 1991–2020 reforecast for the same init month and lead (11 members × 30 years), so drift and bias are removed the same way the C3S models are handled on the ENSO forecasts page. The ECMWF system gets the same treatment on the C3S seasonal models page.

Method

  • Niño-3.4. Monthly index from all 31 members; fan = member 10–90% and 25–75%, sienna line = ensemble mean, observed ERSSTv5 leads in, blue dashes = the C3S seven-model mean from the same issue month. The 3-month toggle gives the ONI convention.
  • Derived indices. IOD (Saji DMI), the South Atlantic Subtropical Dipole (SW − NE), the Niño flavors, and a PDO from North Pacific SST anomalies (global mean removed) projected onto the model's own reforecast EOF1, in reforecast-σ units; same fan and mean.
  • Monthly anomaly maps. One frame per forecast month against the reforecast for the same init month and lead; fixed color scale across leads.
