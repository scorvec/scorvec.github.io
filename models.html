Short-range models

The HRRR, RRFS and RDPS drawn in your browser at each model's own grid spacing (3 km, 3 km and 10 km): simulated infrared, cloud ceiling, visibility, 2 m temperature and dewpoint, vertically integrated smoke, 80 m wind and incoming shortwave. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and hover (or tap) for the model's value.

Runs and timing

Wind and solar plants (shown on the 80 m wind and shortwave maps; at national scale only the larger ones): the U.S. Wind Turbine Database v8.3 (USGS, LBNL and ACP; turbines grouped into projects) and the U.S. Large-Scale Solar PV Database v4.0 (USGS and LBNL), both public domain. Circles and squares scale with capacity; hover for name, capacity and year.

HRRR: every hourly run to 18 h, and the 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC runs to 48 h. RRFS v1: the 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC runs to 84 h (its other hourly runs write only 15-minute surface files to 18 h and are not shown). RDPS: 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC to 84 h. Every forecast hour is shown. The newest run of each model is kept with the one before it (for HRRR also the newest 48-hour run), so Previous run shows how the forecast for the same hour changed. A run appears once its last forecast hour is published; GitHub runs the jobs, so expect the newest HRRR about 1½–2½ hours after its start time.

How the page works

Nothing on this page is a picture. Each field and hour is shipped as the model's own numbers, rounded to 256 levels (0.625 K for infrared, 0.5 °F for temperature and dewpoint, 0.4 m/s for wind, 5 W m−2 for shortwave, and 2–3 % steps on logarithmic scales for ceiling, visibility and smoke) on the native model grid, and your browser colours, projects and reads it: zooming shows the model's grid cells, and the hover value is the stored one, within those steps. Maps are drawn on the HRRR's Lambert conformal projection for all three models, so switching model keeps the view. Boundaries: Natural Earth; US states and counties: US Census via us-atlas.

Related: entraining CAPE from the HRRR and snow-band diagnostics from the HRRR, RRFS and RDPS. Data: NOAA HRRR and RRFS (AWS open data; NOMADS for the RRFS), ECCC RDPS and RAQDPS-FireWork (MSC Datamart). Model output as published, uncorrected.
