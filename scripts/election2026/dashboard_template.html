Method

The House forecast starts from a weighted generic-ballot average (recency half-life 18 days, sample-size weights, likely-voter adjustment, down-weighting of high-undecided polls), simulates the true national margin with historical polling bias and four months of drift, and converts margin to seats through a seats–votes curve. Undecideds are assumed to split evenly in expectation; the average undecided share adds forecast variance instead of being allocated to either party. The Senate forecast simulates all 35 races from per-race market prices with a shared national shock correlating outcomes. Headline probabilities blend the structural model (50%) with Kalshi and Polymarket control markets (25% each).

Built by Claude

Treat it as an AI-built experiment: the methodology is documented and the code is public, but no human statistician has reviewed the calibration.
