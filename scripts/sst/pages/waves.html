Tropical waves

The waves that organise tropical rain: Kelvin waves, equatorial Rossby waves, mixed Rossby–gravity waves and the MJO. The spectrum says which of them are stronger than normal right now; the Hovmöllers and trackers show where each one is along the equator and where the forecast takes it. The MJO has its own page, MJO forecast.
