The plume — all models, every member

The shaded fan is the percentile spread across all members of all models (10–90% and 25–75%); coloured lines are each model's ensemble mean; the bold white line is the multi-model mean (each model weighted equally, so the big NCEP and BoM ensembles don't dominate). Toggle RONI to subtract the tropical background — the honest measure of how strong this event would feel — and members to see every individual trajectory. The observed index leads in from the left. Dotted lines mark ±0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 °C.

Against every ENSO year since 1970

Each grey line is one calendar year of observed ONI; the shaded band is the record envelope (the highest and lowest ONI ever observed in each calendar month since 1970). Named El Niño events are coloured by strength — strong and super events start visible, the rest wait in the legend. The current year is drawn bold white, and the gold dashes extend it with this issue's multi-model mean — showing at a glance whether the forecast would rewrite the record book or just visit it.
