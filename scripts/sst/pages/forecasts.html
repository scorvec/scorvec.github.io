C3S Multi-Model Seasonal Forecast — ONI & RONI

ENSO outlook from the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) multi-system seasonal ensemble — one trajectory per originating centre: ECMWF (SEAS5), the UK Met Office (GloSea6), Météo-France, DWD (GCFS), NCEP (CFSv2), ECCC (CanSIPS, formerly shown alone here) and the Australian Bureau of Meteorology (ACCESS-S2). The upper panel is the traditional ONI (the Niño-3.4 SST anomaly, 3-month running mean); the lower panel is the RONI (Relative Oceanic Niño Index, CPC/ECMWF method — the Niño-3.4 anomaly minus the 20°S–20°N tropical-mean anomaly, rescaled per calendar month so it keeps ONI's °C thresholds). RONI strips out the background tropical warming that inflates the traditional index. Each coloured line is that model's ensemble mean, the bold black line is the multi-model mean, and the grey band is the model-to-model spread. Every model is anomalized against its own 1993–2016 hindcast, which removes each system's drift and bias. Dotted lines mark the ±0.5/1.0/1.5 °C thresholds.
