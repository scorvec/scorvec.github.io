* Graded on RONI: |RONI| ≥ 0.5 El Niño or La Niña, ≥ 1.0 moderate, ≥ 1.5 strong, ≥ 2.0 very strong. CPC adopted RONI as its official ENSO index in February 2026, replacing ONI, whose 30-year base, updated only every five years, lags the tropical background warming — so grading on RONI follows CPC. What is not CPC's here: the value is computed from NOAA OISST v2.1 on a fixed 1991–2020 base, an estimate of CPC's official RONI, which uses ERSSTv6; and a single season is shown, where CPC requires five consecutive overlapping seasons past ±0.5 before declaring an episode. Nothing here is final. CPC revises its own ONI for up to two months after first posting; our newest season leans on a month that has not closed; and the OISST daily field is itself preliminary for about two weeks before it is archived as final.
