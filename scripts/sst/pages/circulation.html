Global circulation and jets

The atmosphere's response to the tropical ocean and what it does downstream: the upper-level flow, angular momentum and its torques, the overturning cells and the jets — one figure at a time, picked from the menu. Every figure carries its method and sources under the caption. Most diagnostics use the full 50-member AIFS-ENS or member 0 alone; the heavier ones (angular momentum, jets, wave activity flux) use a 25-member subset, and each About block says which. The polar vortex, E–P flux, heat flux and the sudden-warming history are on Stratosphere and the polar vortex.
