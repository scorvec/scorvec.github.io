How to read this page

How to read this page. Pick a basin (or a division from the list), the model whose members and band to draw, and a forecast period. Units switches every table and the map between percent of the 1991–2020 normal, the departure from normal in mm, and absolute mm. Issue steps back through earlier runs of the chosen model. Click a division on the map for its cumulative member plumes; click a scorecard row to open that basin below.
