How the update works

The forecast’s simulations share national, state, urban and demographic-group swings. Each simulation is weighted by how closely it matches every count so far: the final margin is taken to be the current margin plus a count error whose spread is about ±28 points with 1% counted and ±3 points with 90% counted, with heavy tails so one odd batch cannot take over. AP calls drop the simulations that disagree. The “final forecast” figures here are this page’s own re-run of the forecast’s simulations before any count, so they can differ from the forecast page by simulation noise (a few tenths of a point). Counts are read by hand from news results pages during the night, so they trail the live tallies by up to 15 minutes. This page is automated and unreviewed; it is not a race call. Margins are Democratic minus Republican, in points.
