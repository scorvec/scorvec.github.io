Turnout tracking, not a forecast. None of this feeds the forecast. Who votes early says little about who wins: in 2022 the early vote leaned Democratic and the Election Day vote leaned Republican in many states. Party figures are voters' registration, not their vote, and states count and report differently, so compare a state with itself over time, not with other states.
