Where the ECMWF ensembles rain too much or too little: the Americas

How much more or less rain the ECMWF ensembles forecast than fell, cell by cell over North and South America, by lead time and season. ECMWF publishes no reforecast with its open data, so each model's rain climate is built here from the operational archive: every 00Z run, six members, one model cycle at a time.

Did the upgrades change the bias?

IFS went from cycle 48r1 to 49r1 on 12 November 2024 and to 50r1 on 12 May 2026, when AIFS-ENS also went from v1 to v2. No field on this page mixes cycles. To see what an upgrade changed, the new cycle's runs are set against the old cycle's runs on the same calendar days a year earlier. The weather differed between the two years, so part of any change is the weather; intervals are 95 % (21-day block bootstrap over the matched days).

  • IFS-ENS 48r1 to 49r1 (257 matched runs). Week 1: bias pattern correlation r = 0.56, 0 % of cells changed significantly, domain-mean bias not significantly changed (+0.06 [−0.03, +0.15] mm/day); week 2: bias pattern correlation r = 0.52, 0 % of cells changed significantly, domain-mean bias not significantly changed (+0.08 [0.00, +0.17] mm/day).
  • IFS-ENS 49r1 to 50r1 (153 matched runs). Week 1: bias pattern correlation r = 0.45, 6 % of cells changed significantly, domain-mean bias changed by +0.19 mm/day [+0.15, +0.23]; week 2: bias pattern correlation r = 0.38, 7 % of cells changed significantly, domain-mean bias changed by +0.22 mm/day [+0.11, +0.34].
  • AIFS-ENS v1 to v2 (103 matched runs). Week 1: bias pattern correlation r = 0.55, 10 % of cells changed significantly, domain-mean bias changed by +0.22 mm/day [+0.13, +0.30]; week 2: bias pattern correlation r = 0.49, 11 % of cells changed significantly, domain-mean bias changed by +0.21 mm/day [+0.05, +0.37].
  • The two May 2026 upgrades on the same days, IFS-ENS's change minus AIFS-ENS's: week 1 −0.04 mm/day [−0.05, −0.02], while each model changed by about 0.21; week 2 0.00 mm/day [−0.03, +0.03], not significant, while each model changed by about 0.22. The weather year cancels in this difference, so most of the change both models share is the weather year (or a change in IMERG Late), not either upgrade.

How it is measured

  • Forecast: the mean of members 1-6 of each 00Z run, accumulated over the lead band, as 1° box means. Cycles: IFS-ENS 48r1 2024-03-01 to 2024-11-12, 49r1 2024-11-13 to 2026-05-12, 50r1 2026-05-13 to now; AIFS-ENS v1 2025-07-02 to 2026-05-12, v2 2026-05-13 to now.
  • Truth: IMERG V07 Late, the satellite record that keeps up with the forecasts, corrected by its own seasonal difference from IMERG Final, which carries the monthly gauge adjustment over land (2024-03-01 to 2025-09-30). The size of that correction is on the last map.
  • Significance: runs a few days apart share their weather, so each mean's uncertainty comes from resampling whole 21-day blocks of start dates; a cell is shaded only if it passes a false-discovery-rate test at q = 0.10 across the map.
  • Domain: 60°S-60°N, 170°W-30°W. IMERG is unreliable over snow and ice and poleward of 60°, so Alaska and northern Canada are left out.
