Teleconnections — NAO · PNA · EPO · WPO · AO

Computed in-house, not fetched: a varimax-rotated principal-component analysis of our own ERA5 Z500 monthly anomaly history (1991–2020 base, 20–90N, √cos-weighted) yields the loading patterns — classical centers of action are used only to name and orient the modes — and AO is the unrotated leading EOF (the annular mode). Daily indices are projections of each day's anomaly field onto those patterns, standardized by day-of-year. Table shows the last 13 months with values; the chart below is the full monthly history — click legend entries to toggle.

Method & sources: ERA5 daily means computed from the hourly ARCO-ERA5 archive (~5-day lag); anomalies vs a 1991–2020 daily climatology (mean + 4 annual harmonics per gridpoint) built from WeatherBench2's conservatively regridded 1.5° ERA5.
