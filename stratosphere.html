Stratosphere and the polar vortex

Whether the winter polar vortex is about to weaken, and what that has meant at the surface before. The forecast side follows the vortex winds, the planetary waves driving them and the heat flux carrying those waves upward, member by member through the 15-day AIFS ensemble. The history side is every winter and every major sudden warming since 1980 from MERRA-2, with only the statistically significant results shown. Background on each diagnostic is in the sudden stratospheric warming, E–P flux and eddy heat flux explainers. The tropospheric circulation is on Global circulation and jets.
