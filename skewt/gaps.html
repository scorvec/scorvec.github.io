US sounding gap report

How often every active US radiosonde site launched on time at 00Z and 12Z since January: ■ on time · ■ off-hour · ■ missed.

About this page

Every US radiosonde site is supposed to launch at 00Z and 12Z, every day. Of late, many haven't — releases skipped outright or shifted to off-hours. This page grades every routine slot since the start of the year for every active US station: ■ on time · ■ off-hour launch instead · ■ missed (no sounding in IGRA: not launched, or launched but not archived). Stations with a clean 60-day record are collapsed at the bottom.

Data: NOAA IGRA v2 year-to-date files (≈2-day lag) · a slot counts as "off-hour instead" when no 00Z/12Z launch exists but a non-routine launch fell nearer that slot than the other one. Built by Claude (Anthropic's Claude Fable 5), directed by Shawn Corvec.
