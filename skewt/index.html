Kinematics & composites

Data: latest soundings from the University of Wyoming archive (BUFR, mirrored ~4×/day — UW sends no CORS headers); any historical date directly from NOAA IGRA v2 (first load per station can be tens of MB, then cached). Analysis: SHARPlib (K. Halbert, NWS SPC lineage) as WebAssembly — SB/ML/MU parcels, LI, DCAPE, effective inflow, Bunkers, SRH, effective shear, SCP/STP. Map: Leaflet & OpenStreetMap.
