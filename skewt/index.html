Global Real-time & Archived Sounding Explorer

Every weather-balloon (radiosonde) sounding ever released, anywhere on Earth, back more than a century. Blue stations reported within the last 24 h; purple ones launched off-hour (06/18Z) — usually a special release ahead of severe weather; teal are active, red are closed (archive only). Click a station, then pick Latest or Archive and a date. Recent launches load instantly; the first click on a station can take a few seconds to load, or longer for archived data which has to be downloaded (older soundings come from NOAA's global archive as one big per-station file, with a pulsing note showing progress) — then every other date at that station is instant. The full analysis — parcels, CAPE, shear, hodograph — runs entirely in your browser via SHARPlib compiled to WebAssembly. READ THIS FIRST! →  ·  US sounding gap report →
