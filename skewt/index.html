Parcels

Kinematics & composites

Data: radiosonde profiles via the University of Wyoming sounding archive (BUFR high-resolution), mirrored ~4×/day by a GitHub Action because UW sends no CORS headers — every station with a launch in the last 36 hours is clickable (blue dots). Archive mode pulls any historical date directly from NOAA IGRA v2 (grey dots include closed stations; per-station files can be tens of MB on first load, then cached — recent years use the small year-to-date files). Analysis: SHARPlib (K. Halbert, NWS SPC lineage) compiled to WebAssembly — surface / 100-hPa mixed-layer / most-unstable parcels (Wobus lifter), effective inflow layer, Bunkers storm motion, SRH, effective shear, SCP & STP. Diagram: log-p / 45° skew, dry & moist adiabats traced with the same lifter. Map: Leaflet & OpenStreetMap. Built by Claude (Anthropic's Claude Fable 5), directed by Shawn Corvec. Not for operational use.
