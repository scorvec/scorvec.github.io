Privacy

Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses; the totals are public on the stats page. There are no adverts and no other trackers. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, scripts from jsDelivr and cdnjs, map tiles from Esri and some live data straight from its providers (the Iowa Environmental Mesonet and the National Weather Service, for example). Like the hosts that serve the site and its figures, those services see a visitor's IP address when the browser fetches from them.

How it is made

The products are built by automated pipelines in GitHub Actions from open model and observation data and are published without review by a person. The code is written with Claude, Anthropic's AI model. The studies and explainers are written by Claude at my direction, say so in their bylines and are not peer reviewed. Errors in the data, figures or text can be reported as an issue on GitHub.
